How the score works

  • +15 per past no-show (max 45)
  • +15 if booked more than 14 days ahead (+8 if over 7)
  • +10 if more than 25 km away
  • +8 for new patients, +6 for ages under 30
  • −15 if the patient confirmed

Rule-based by design so every score is explainable to clinic staff. Not medical advice.
